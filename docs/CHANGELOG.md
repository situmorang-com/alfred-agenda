# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 16 Nov 2026

The first public release.

- `cal` puts the meeting that matters now on top, with a countdown. ↩ joins it in Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and more, from 15 minutes before the start.
- `day` shows a day with its free gaps, ready to block; `week` and `month` are calendar grids.
- `cal +` adds an event from plain words (`cal + design sync 11:30`), showing what it overlaps before you press ↩.
- `cal -` deletes an event after a confirmation whose first row keeps it. Meetings with other people, invitations and read-only calendars are never offered.
- Meeting alerts: a minute before a meeting with a link, Alfred opens the join list. While the screen is locked you get a notification instead, and the list after you unlock.
- One package for Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 14 or later.

## 1.0.0 — 13 Nov 2026
